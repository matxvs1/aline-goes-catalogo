# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a small vanilla script, generated by `gerar.py` from `modelo.html`. Hosted on GitHub Pages (user decision, 2026-11-18). No framework, no server.

## Users

Primary: crocheteiras (women who crochet) who follow Aline on Instagram and arrive from the link in her bio, almost always on a phone. Their job is to find a piece they want to make and get to the place where the lesson lives.

Secondary: people who want a finished piece made to order.

## Product Purpose

One catalog page that replaces the Canva link page in Aline's Instagram bio. It lists every lesson, tutorial and made-to-order piece and sends the visitor to the right destination: the paid lesson checkout, the YouTube lesson, the Instagram tutorial, or WhatsApp for an order. Success is a visitor reaching the lesson she wanted in a couple of taps.

## Positioning

Every piece is an original design by Aline Goes, and she teaches each one herself. The catalog is the single place where paid lessons, free lessons and quick tutorials sit side by side.

## Operating Context

- Entry point is the Instagram bio link (`bit.ly/infor_donamiau`), today a Canva page with five lesson buttons.
- Paid lessons are sold on Kiwify. Free lessons live on her YouTube channel (`@Donamiiau`) and on the Fischer Fios YouTube channel. Quick tutorials are Instagram reels.
- Orders are arranged person to person (WhatsApp, Instagram Direct).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 42 pieces in four groups: 6 paid lessons, 12 free lessons, 18 tutorials, 6 made-to-order pieces. Three families: bags, accessories, clothing.
- Filter by group and by family, search by name, and a detail sheet per piece with description, materials and the action button.
- Prices are not shown: current prices were not confirmed.
- Direct video links for the 12 free lessons are not known yet. Open decision: Aline supplies them.
- The split into families and into "tutorial" versus "free lesson" was inferred from her captions. Open decision: Aline confirms.

## Brand Commitments

None fixed. The user released name treatment, colour and style for a new proposal (2026-11-18). The facts stay: the names "Aline Goes" and "Dona Miau", the term "crochêgenheira" she uses for herself, and her tagline "Entre técnica, criatividade e propósito".

## Evidence on Hand

- 119 Instagram posts with captions, collected 2026-11-18. Cover images in `dados/capas/`.
- Product photos from the Kiwify checkout pages in `img/`.
- Real links: six Kiwify checkouts, WhatsApp (82) 98163-7859, YouTube `@Donamiiau`, Instagram `@donamiiau`.
- Not on hand, never to be fabricated: prices, testimonials, student counts, clean product photography for most pieces (covers carry her own lettering).

## Product Principles

1. Truth only: every description comes from her own captions.
2. The piece is the hero. The page gets out of the way of the crochet.
3. Two taps to the lesson.
4. Phone first, because the visitor arrives from Instagram.
5. Paid lessons are the business and get the best seat.
